ZOMBIE WARD

First-person zombie survival

Difficulty

Weaker, slower zombies that hit softly and come in smaller waves.
The intended experience.
Tougher, faster, harder-hitting zombies in bigger waves.

Tutorials

Touch controls

  • Left thumb drags a stick to move. Push it all the way to run.
  • Right thumb drags anywhere to look. Hold FIRE to shoot, and keep dragging that thumb to aim.
  • AIM toggles the rifle scope. Aim assist gently pulls your view toward zombies near the crosshair.
  • JUMP, DUCK (tap while running to slide), RELOAD, GRENADE, and the weapon button cycles knife, pistol, rifle.
  • USE does what E does below: tap at doors, windows and the ammo crate, hold to repair boards. Hold JUMP to climb the ladder.
  • The II button pauses. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen for full-screen play.

The basics

  • Click Start, then click in the game to capture your mouse. Press Esc to pause.
  • Move with WASD, look with the mouse, jump with Space.
  • Hold Shift to sprint. Caps Lock crouches. Press Caps Lock while sprinting to slide.
  • Your health bar is bottom-left. Stop attacking for 6.5 seconds and you slowly heal.
  • The round minimap (top-right) rotates with you: red = zombies, blue = allies, yellow = ammo crate, green = pickups.

Combat

  • 1 knife, 2 pistol, 3 rifle. Hold left click to fire, R to reload.
  • Right click aims the rifle through its scope — zoomed and more accurate, but slower.
  • G throws a grenade (you start with 2). More come from zombie kills — about one every other wave — or from the ammo crate.
  • Zombies are tough: aim for centre mass and use grenades on crowds.

Defending the house

  • Zombies attack the boarded door and windows first. Watch the health bars above them.
  • Hold E at a damaged board to repair it. Tap E at the door to lock or unlock it, or at a window to pry it open or board it up.
  • If you lock yourself out you need the house key — it's lying somewhere in the yard.
  • The ammo crate refills guns and grenades and moves to a new spot every wave. Press E next to it.
  • Climb the ladder on the side of the house (hold Space) to reach the roof, where two allies cover the yard.

When you turn

  • If your health hits zero you don't die — you turn into a zombie, and so do your allies.
  • The fence comes down and you can roam the whole neighborhood. Survivors hide among the houses and shoot back.
  • You keep your weapons and grenades. F is a bite that infects instantly; kills with weapons count too.
  • Your zombie squad follows you and hunts survivors on its own. If the survivors kill you now, it's over.

How to win

  • As a human: survive 30 waves.
  • As a zombie: infect 100 survivors.
